Hindustani Classical Vocal

Develop the fundamentals of Hindustani vocal music through structured lessons and practice.

Children's Music Classes

A welcoming introduction to Indian classical music designed around a child's age and learning pace.

  • Age-appropriate fundamentals
  • Listening and singing exercises
  • Rhythm and pitch awareness
  • Gradual introduction to ragas
  • Confidence through practice

Online Classes

  • Individual or small-group options
  • Flexible learning from different locations
  • Regular practice guidance
  • Progressive curriculum
