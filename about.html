OUR STORY

A space to learn, practice, and enjoy music.

HindustaniSangeet is envisioned as a welcoming place for students to discover Hindustani classical music and develop a lasting relationship with the art form.

Learning can begin with the fundamentals and grow naturally through regular practice, listening, and guided exploration of ragas and rhythm.

OUR APPROACH

Tradition with a thoughtful learning experience.

Strong foundations

Start with swara, basic patterns, voice culture, rhythm, and listening.

Progressive learning

Build skills step by step as musical understanding and confidence grow.

Individual attention

Lessons can be adapted to a student's age, experience, pace, and goals.
